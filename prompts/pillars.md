# Content Pillars (Bible §5)

Four pillars, one clean taxonomy the calendar runs on. The `share` is the target mix
across a week/month — the calendar should balance toward these percentages.

| Pillar (enum) | Share | Purpose | Example topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| `practical_ai_for_operators` | **35%** | Teach practical AI & automation use cases. | 3 workflows every SME should automate first. |
| `proof_and_use_cases` | **25%** | Show credibility with real client work & systems. | How document processing went from hours to minutes. |
| `founder_build_notes` | **25%** | Humanize the brand; build trust in public. | What we learned building our own content pipeline. |
| `ai_readiness_and_offer` | **15%** | Prepare buyers and drive the call. | The 7 signs your business is ready for AI automation. |

**Trends are a lens, not a pillar (§5):** timely trend/opinion content is applied
*within* the four pillars so we stay relevant without chasing news that doesn't serve
the ICP.

## Content philosophy gate (§2.2)
Every post must answer **at least one**. If it answers none, it does not ship:
1. Does this attract our ideal customer?
2. Does this build trust in Pratap AI?
3. Does this teach something useful?
4. Does this connect to an offer or next step?
5. Can this be repurposed?
6. Can we support the claim with proof?
