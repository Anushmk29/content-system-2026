# ICP — The Governing Layer (Bible §3)

> **READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE.** The ICP is the spine of the entire content
> system. Every topic, post, hook, and CTA is checked against it. If a piece of
> content does not serve the people defined here, it does not ship — no matter how
> good it looks.

Every agent loads this file as context. The QC gate rejects content that fails the
"audience fit" check (§10.3) against it.

## Two layers — one voice

| Layer | What it is | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary ICP** | Founder-led SMEs & service businesses with manual workflows. | The default voice. **~85% of content** speaks directly to this layer. |
| **Aspirational ICP** | Larger SMEs, 100–500 employees, $2M–$20M, multiple teams. | Informs research and a few strategic posts — **never** the default tone. |

**Voice guardrail:** the Aspirational layer brings in words like "governance," "ROI
and risk," and "process mapping." Those are *research inputs, not our publishing
voice.* We do not serve enterprises, and our content should never sound like we do.

## Primary ICP — who they are (§3.2)
Founder-led SMEs and service businesses that:
- Have manual workflows.
- Depend heavily on founder or operator memory.
- Use spreadsheets, WhatsApp, CRMs, and scattered tools.
- Know AI can help but do not know where to start.
- Want practical systems, not complex enterprise transformation.

## The people we write for (§3.2)
| Role | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Founder / Owner | Growth, control, speed, visibility. |
| CEO / Managing Director | Efficiency, delegation, better systems. |
| Operations Head | Process clarity, automation, accountability. |
| Sales Head | Lead capture, follow-up, CRM discipline. |
| Marketing Head | Content consistency, campaign execution. |
| Real Estate Operator | Lead flow, follow-up, property data, team coordination. |

## Pain → content angle we own (§3.4)
| Pain they feel | Content angle |
|---|---|
| Work scattered across too many tools. | Company OS / dashboard content. |
| Want AI but don't know where to start. | AI readiness content. |
| Team wastes time on repetitive tasks. | Automation workflow content. |
| Leads falling through the cracks. | CRM + follow-up automation. |
| Content is inconsistent. | AI-assisted content pipeline. |
